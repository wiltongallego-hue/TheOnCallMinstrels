## Intent

Turn the Gemini-generated gameplay clip of The On-Call Minstrels into a seamless loop of the
wizard walking through the level: "Transforme esse vídeo em um loop do personagem andando pela
fase, de maneira que ao chegar no fim do vídeo, ele se conecte ao início do vídeo."

The original camera only travels one way (fireplace → map), so the loop is a patrol:
the original footage plays forward, the wizard stops on the map and turns, then walks back
(jumping over the same spots) while the camera retraces its path, and he stops at the fireplace
where the loop begins again.

## Assets

- assets/bg/hud_*.webp — original gameplay frames 142–212 and 143–153 with the score HUD removed.
- assets/bg/full_*.webp — reconstructed clean plates (wizard + HUD removed) for the walk back.
- assets/bg/v_decel_*.webp, assets/bg/v_accel_*.webp — virtual-camera plates for the stop/turn.
- assets/sprites/s*.webp, assets/sprites/b*.webp — RGBA wizard sprites (full / body-only).
- ../../remotion/public/timeline.json — the per-frame timeline this composition is generated from.

## Notes

- Silent loop (the source music does not loop at this length).
- Same timeline is rendered by the Remotion project in `remotion/`; both must stay frame-identical.
- Generated by `tools/loop_pipeline/build_hyperframes.py`; edit the timeline, not index.html.
